# Rubric: Evidence and methodological rigor (0–100)

Assess the adequacy of the methodological evidence VISIBLE IN THE RECORD:
validation (internal/external), explicit assumptions, uncertainty assessment (PSA,
credible intervals, scenario analysis), calibration targets and approach, transparency and
reproducibility signals (code/data availability, reporting guidelines such as CHEERS,
STROBE, TRIPOD), and appropriateness of methods to the question.

Rules:
- Do NOT reward journal prestige. Do NOT penalize a preprint for being a preprint.
- Absence of a detail in an abstract is not evidence of absence in the paper: when key
  elements cannot be judged, score near the middle (40–60), add a limitation
  "insufficient methods information in abstract", and set confidence ≤ 0.4.
- High scores (≥ 80) require explicit evidence in the record (e.g. "externally validated",
  "probabilistic sensitivity analysis", "calibrated to …").
