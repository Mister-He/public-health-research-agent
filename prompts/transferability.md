# Rubric: Cross-disciplinary transferability (0–100)

How plausibly could the paper's METHOD be transferred to the research profile's problems,
especially when the paper comes from outside public health?

Examples of high transferability:
- reinforcement learning → adaptive or risk-stratified screening policies;
- knowledge graphs → evidence synthesis for model parameterization;
- retrieval-augmented generation → querying a local scientific library;
- scalable agent-based computation → infectious disease simulation in high-risk populations;
- emulator-based calibration from engineering/climate → microsimulation calibration.

Anchors: 80–100 direct, concrete transfer path; 50–79 plausible with adaptation;
20–49 weak/indirect; 0–19 none. A paper already squarely within public health modelling
scores by how reusable its method is elsewhere in the profile (not automatically high).
Explain the transfer path in `rationale` (marked as interpretation).
